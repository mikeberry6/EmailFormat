Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • April 18 – April 24, 2026
KEY THEMES
Eighteen deals across all six sectors marked the busiest week of the year, with three transactions clearing $1bn — CVC’s $2.3bn SubCom continuation vehicle, the KKR-led $1.5bn Vertical Bridge round, and Ares’s X-Energy Nasdaq IPO.
Sponsor monetizations dominated the tape: CVC’s SubCom continuation vehicle, CPP Investments’ divestiture of Cordelio’s 6GW portfolio to BrightNight, KKR’s sale of its Pembina Gas stake to Apollo, and GIC’s €573mm Teréga exit to Enagás.
Power & ET 5 Deals
X-Energy | Ares
Ares (IPO) · Advanced Nuclear · US
X-Energy, a portfolio company of Ares Management, priced its upsized initial public offering and commenced public trading on the Nasdaq. The target is a developer of advanced small modular nuclear reactors and proprietary fuel technologies.

Green Energy Storage Initiative (GESI) | Allianz
Allianz (Majority Stake) · Battery Storage · Europe
Allianz Global Investors definitively acquired a 51% equity stake in the Green Energy Storage Initiative. The target is a German utility-scale battery storage platform.
Source
 
Cordelio Power’s 6GW Joint Venture Interest | CPP Investments
CPP Investments / Cordelio Power (Divestiture) · Renewable Energy · US
Cordelio Power, a wholly-owned portfolio company of CPP Investments, formally divested its joint venture equity interest to BrightNight. The target is a 6GW renewable energy development portfolio located in the Western United States.
Source
 
Alliance Energy Group Portfolio | Partners Group
Partners Group / PowerTransitions (Bolt-On) · Power Generation · US
PowerTransitions, a portfolio company of Partners Group, signed a definitive agreement to acquire the Alliance Energy Group Portfolio. The target comprises five operating power plants totaling 323 MW of generation capacity.
Source
 
FPH2’s Los Angeles County Renewable Hydrogen Project | Quinbrook
Quinbrook / Elemental Clean Fuels (Bolt-On) · Renewable Hydrogen · US
Elemental Clean Fuels, an energy transition platform backed by Quinbrook Infrastructure Partners, agreed to finance and construct FPH2’s renewable hydrogen project. The target is a priority solar-powered electrolytic hydrogen facility located in Los Angeles County.
Source
Digital 5 Deals
Subsea Communications (SubCom) | CVC
CVC (Secondary) · Subsea Cables · Global
CVC Secondary Partners led a $2.3bn equity investment into a single-asset continuation vehicle for Subsea Communications. The target is a leading turnkey supplier of subsea fiber optic cable systems.
Source
 
Vertical Bridge | KKR, DigitalBridge & CDPQ
KKR, DigitalBridge & CDPQ (Growth Equity) · Telecom Towers · US
KKR led a newly announced $1.5bn strategic equity investment round in Vertical Bridge alongside participation from existing sponsors DigitalBridge and CDPQ. The target is the largest private owner and operator of wireless communications towers in the United States.
Source
 
EdgeConneX | EQT
EQT (Seed Transfer) · Data Centers · Global
EQT executed an internal equity transfer to acquire a minority seed stake in EdgeConneX for its newly launched AI Infrastructure strategy. The target is a premier global data center platform.
Source
 
Spectrotel & AireSpring | Charlesbank
Charlesbank (Strategic Investment) · Fiber Networks · US
Charlesbank Capital Partners led a new strategic equity investment to back the combination and merger of Spectrotel and AireSpring. The combined target operates nationwide facility-based fiber networks and provides managed connectivity solutions.
Source
 
SiFi Networks America LLC | Patrizia
Patrizia (Buyout) · Fiber Networks · US
Patrizia completed a direct buyout to acquire the remaining equity interests from the founders of SiFi Networks America LLC. The target is a developer of open-access, citywide 100% fiber optic networks.
Source
Midstream 3 Deals
Pembina Gas Infrastructure (PGI) | Apollo & KKR
Apollo (Stake Acquisition) & KKR (Divestiture) · Gas Processing · Canada
Apollo Funds formally reached a definitive agreement to acquire KKR’s 40% equity stake in Pembina Gas Infrastructure. The target is a premier midstream gas processing entity and gathering pipeline network in Western Canada.
Source
 
Teréga S.A.S. | GIC
GIC (Divestiture) · Gas Transmission & Storage · Europe
GIC directly executed a definitive agreement to divest its 31.5% equity stake in Teréga S.A.S. to Enagás for €573mm. The target is a French midstream gas transmission and storage operator.
Source
 
Kinetik Holdings Inc. | I Squared
I Squared (Divestiture) · Natural Gas Pipelines · US
I Squared Capital executed an open-market divestiture of a portion of its minority equity stake in Kinetik Holdings via block trades. The target is a midstream energy and natural gas pipeline operator.
Source

Stonepeak (Platform Acquisition) · Marinas · US
Stonepeak completed a direct equity recapitalization and buyout of Southern Marinas from KSL Capital Partners. The target is a premier operator of marine infrastructure and marinas across the United States.

F3 Marina | Stonepeak
Stonepeak / Southern Marinas (Bolt-On) · Marinas · US
Southern Marinas, an infrastructure portfolio company of Stonepeak, concurrently completed the bolt-on equity acquisition of F3 Marina. The target is a 59,000-square-foot specialist marina facility located in Fort Lauderdale.
