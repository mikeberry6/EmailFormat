PJM Generation Portfolio (4.4 GW) | LS Power
LS Power (Buyout) · Thermal Power · US
LS Power agreed to acquire approximately 4.4 GW of predominantly natural gas–fired generation capacity in PJM from Constellation Energy for $5bn, representing ~$1,142/kW. The portfolio includes the Bethlehem, York 1, York 2, Hay Road, and Edge Moor facilities in Delaware and Pennsylvania. The sale is the largest portion of the divestitures required by DOJ as part of the antitrust resolution of Constellation’s Calpine acquisition. Santander is advising LS Power; Barclays and SMBC/Jefferies are advising Constellation.

Plenitude (Eni) | Ares Management
Ares Management (Growth Equity) · Renewables · Italy
Eni announced a non-proportional capital increase of approximately €1.5bn for Plenitude, its renewables and retail energy unit, with at least €1bn provided by existing shareholder Ares Management. The raise is based on a 100% pre-money equity valuation of €10.75bn (implied EV of €13.1bn). The transaction establishes joint control between Eni (~65% post-raise) and Ares under a new governance framework, resulting in Plenitude’s deconsolidation from Eni’s financial statements. Energy Infrastructure Partners retains its 10% stake. Plenitude targets 15 GW installed capacity and 15mm retail customers by 2030 (5.8 GW and €2.5bn EBITDA as of YE 2025). Mediobanca is advising Eni.
Source
 
174 MW Wind Portfolio (France) | Axium Infrastructure Europe
Axium Infrastructure Europe (Minority Stake) · Wind · France
Axium Infrastructure Europe acquired a 49.9% equity interest in an operational wind portfolio totaling 174.2 MW from la Banque des Territoires (Caisse des Dépôts). ENGIE Green retains the remaining 50.1% interest. The portfolio comprises 22 wind projects across Hauts-de-France (79.4 MW), Nouvelle-Aquitaine (52.8 MW), and Grand Est (42.0 MW), benefiting from stable contracted revenues through French government-backed Feed-in-Tariff and Contract-for-Difference schemes.

Transportation 2 Deals
PMI Electro / Allfleet | KKR
KKR (Growth Equity) · EV Fleet · India
KKR agreed to invest up to $310mm in a dual-stake structure comprising a majority stake in Allfleet, an e-bus fleet ownership and operations platform, and a minority stake in PMI Electro Mobility Solutions, India’s leading electric bus manufacturer with 2,900+ battery-electric buses operating across 31 cities. Capital will fund Allfleet’s urban fleet scale-up and PMI Electro’s manufacturing expansion, supporting deployment of 5,000+ e-buses under long-term concession agreements. This is the first deployment of KKR’s Global Climate Transition strategy in India and the eighth globally.
Source
 
Revolv | Zenōbē
Zenōbē (Buyout) · EV Fleet · US
Zenōbē, backed by KKR, Infracapital, and Mubadala, acquired California-based Revolv from GDEV Management to enter the US electric truck market. The acquisition adds 13 operational customer sites, 100+ electric trucks, and a development pipeline. Zenōbē now supports 3,400+ vehicles globally and is targeting expansion into Illinois, New York, and Massachusetts. This represents the first company exit from GDEV’s second fund.
Source
Digital 1 Deal
NorthC Datacenters | Antin Infrastructure Partners
Antin Infrastructure Partners (Buyout) · Data Centers · Netherlands
Antin Infrastructure Partners completed its acquisition of NorthC Datacenters from DWS and minority shareholders, the sixth investment from Antin’s €10.2bn Flagship Fund V. NorthC operates 25 colocation data centers across the Netherlands, Germany, and Switzerland with 140+ MW of secured gross grid capacity, serving 1,600+ enterprise customers. Press reports valued NorthC at up to €2bn. Proceeds will support NorthC’s next expansion phase, including new greenfield data centers in Frankfurt, Basel, and Geneva to meet growing demand for AI inference workloads.
Source
Midstream 1 Deal
MidOcean Energy | EIG
EIG / Idemitsu Kosan (Growth Equity) · LNG · Global
MidOcean Energy, an LNG platform formed and managed by EIG, announced an oversubscribed initial equity raise of $1.2bn against a $1bn target, with a cumulative target of up to $2bn. The raise was anchored by a $500mm commitment from Japan’s Idemitsu Kosan, marking its strategic entry into global LNG. MidOcean holds interests in LNG Canada, Gorgon LNG, Pluto LNG, QCLNG, and Peru LNG, and recently agreed to acquire additional Gorgon interest from JERA.
