Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • March 14 – March 20, 2026
KEY THEMES
Muted sponsor M&A activity this week at 6 deals, down from 17 last week. The marquee transaction is LS Power’s $5bn acquisition of 4.4 GW of PJM gas-fired generation from Constellation Energy — a forced divestiture arising from the DOJ/FERC antitrust resolution of Constellation’s Calpine acquisition, highlighting the growing regulatory complexity of large-scale power sector consolidation.
The Constellation divestiture underscores thermal gas generation’s enduring strategic value at ~$1,142/kW, even as policy momentum tilts toward renewables. Elsewhere, Ares scaled its Plenitude position via a €1.5bn capital increase, KKR deployed $310mm into Indian e-bus infrastructure, and Antin closed the NorthC Datacenters buyout from its €10.2bn Flagship Fund V.
Power & ET 3 Deals
PJM Generation Portfolio (4.4 GW) | LS Power
LS Power (Buyout) · Thermal Power · US
LS Power agreed to acquire ~4.4 GW of predominantly gas-fired generation capacity in PJM from Constellation Energy for $5bn (~$1,142/kW). The portfolio includes the Bethlehem, York 1, York 2, Hay Road, and Edge Moor facilities in Delaware and Pennsylvania. The sale satisfies the largest portion of the DOJ divestitures required for Constellation’s Calpine acquisition.

Plenitude (Eni) | Ares Management
Ares Management (Growth Equity) · Renewables · Italy
Eni announced a ~€1.5bn non-proportional capital increase for Plenitude, its renewables and retail energy unit, with at least €1bn from existing shareholder Ares Management (pre-money equity valuation of €10.75bn). The transaction establishes joint control between Eni (~65% post-raise) and Ares, resulting in Plenitude’s deconsolidation from Eni’s financial statements. Energy Infrastructure Partners retains its 10% stake.
Source
 
174 MW Wind Portfolio (France) | Axium Infrastructure Europe
Axium Infrastructure Europe (Minority Stake) · Wind · France
Axium Infrastructure Europe acquired a 49.9% interest in a 174.2 MW operational wind portfolio from la Banque des Territoires (Caisse des Dépôts), with ENGIE Green retaining 50.1%. The portfolio comprises 22 projects across Hauts-de-France, Nouvelle-Aquitaine, and Grand Est, with revenues contracted under French government Feed-in-Tariff and CfD schemes.

Transportation 2 Deals
PMI Electro / Allfleet | KKR
KKR (Growth Equity) · EV Fleet · India
KKR agreed to invest up to $310mm, acquiring a majority stake in Allfleet (e-bus fleet ownership platform) and a minority stake in PMI Electro Mobility Solutions, India’s leading e-bus manufacturer with 2,900+ buses across 31 cities. Capital will fund deployment of 5,000+ e-buses under long-term concession agreements. First deployment of KKR’s Global Climate Transition strategy in India.
Source
 
Revolv | Zenōbē
Zenōbē (Buyout) · EV Fleet · US
Zenōbē, backed by KKR, Infracapital, and Mubadala, acquired California-based Revolv from GDEV Management to enter the US commercial electric truck market. The acquisition adds 13 operational customer sites and 100+ electric trucks. Zenōbē now supports 3,400+ vehicles globally and is targeting expansion into Illinois, New York, and Massachusetts.
Source
Digital 1 Deal
NorthC Datacenters | Antin Infrastructure Partners
Antin Infrastructure Partners (Buyout) · Data Centers · Netherlands
Antin Infrastructure Partners completed its acquisition of NorthC Datacenters from DWS and minority shareholders, the sixth investment from Antin’s €10.2bn Flagship Fund V. NorthC operates 25 colocation data centers across the Netherlands, Germany, and Switzerland with 140+ MW of secured grid capacity, serving 1,600+ enterprise customers. Greenfield expansion planned in Frankfurt, Basel, and Geneva.
