Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • February 14–20, 2026
KEY THEMES
An active week for sponsor deal flow produced eleven transactions across five sectors. Transportation drew attention as Macquarie led a consortium take-private of Qube Holdings, Australia’s largest port operator, while Blackstone’s Infrastructure Fund acquired a 10% stake in the Fraport Greece airport network. Digital infrastructure saw three transactions, headlined by InfraVia’s £850M commitment to acquire DigitalBridge’s UK full-fibre platform via the nexfibre JV, alongside Oaktree’s lead role in Sharon AI’s NASDAQ IPO and Allianz’s entry into ATC Europe’s wireless tower portfolio. Power and energy transition accounted for four deals, including Infratil’s NZ$525M equity raise for Contact Energy and Ara Partners’ co-led $100M Series D for clean hydrogen producer Utility Global.
Power & ET 4 Deals
Contact Energy Generation · New Zealand
Acquirer: Infratil
Infratil formally confirmed its participation in a NZ$525 million follow-on equity raise for Contact Energy. The target operates in power and renewable energy generation.
 
One Power Company Generation · US
Acquirer: OnSite Partners (Basalt Infrastructure Partners)
OnSite Partners, a portfolio company capitalized by Basalt Infrastructure Partners’ Fund IV, formally announced the add-on acquisition of business interests in One Power Company.
 
Utility Global Energy Transition · US
Acquirer: Ara Partners / APG Infrastructure
Ara Partners and APG Infrastructure co-led a $100 million Series D growth equity financing round for Utility Global. The target produces clean hydrogen and decarbonization solutions, fitting the energy transition vertical.
 
Duffy BESS Project Storage · US
Acquirer: Linea Energy (EnCap Energy Transition Fund)
Linea Energy, a battery energy storage portfolio company backed by the EnCap Energy Transition Fund, formally announced a definitive preferred equity financing agreement.
Digital 3 Deals
ATC Europe Towers · Europe
Acquirer: Allianz Global Investors / Commerz Real
Allianz Global Investors, partnering with Commerz Real, announced a definitive agreement to acquire and manage a minority equity stake in ATC Europe. The target is a wireless communications tower operator.
 
Substantial Group (Netomnia) Fibre · UK
Acquirer: InfraVia Capital Partners
Seller: DigitalBridge
A DigitalBridge investment fund announced a definitive agreement to divest its equity stake in the UK full-fibre platform. Concurrently, InfraVia Capital Partners committed £850 million in direct equity to co-acquire the provider via the nexfibre joint venture.
 
Sharon AI Data Centers · US
Acquirer: Oaktree Capital (IPO)
Oaktree Capital acted as the lead institutional investor in the IPO of Sharon AI, with the first day of public trading on the NASDAQ. The target provides cloud GPU platforms, compute infrastructure, and data centers.
Transportation 2 Deals
Qube Holdings Limited Ports · Australia
Acquirer: Macquarie Asset Management (MAM)
A consortium led by Macquarie Asset Management announced a binding Scheme Implementation Deed for a take-private acquisition of Qube Holdings Limited. The target is Australia’s largest provider of ports and terminal infrastructure.
 
Fraport Greece Airports · Greece
Acquirer: Blackstone Infrastructure Strategies Fund
The Blackstone Infrastructure Strategies Fund formally announced the purchase of a 10% minority equity stake in the regional airport network.
Waste & ES 1 Deal
Kalfresh Bioenergy Facility Bioenergy · Australia
Acquirer: QIC Global Infrastructure / Wollemi Capital
QIC Global Infrastructure announced a direct A$80 million joint equity commitment alongside Wollemi Capital to construct the facility. The facility converts agricultural and food waste into renewable electricity and natural gas.
Social 1 Deal
Culmia Affordable Housing Portfolio Housing · Spain
Acquirer: MEAG
MEAG formally announced a direct 50% equity joint venture acquisition of the portfolio located in Spain. The asset consists of 1,800 affordable homes, qualifying as a traditional social infrastructure vertical.
