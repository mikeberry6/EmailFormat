Infrastructure Sponsor M&A Activity
Weekly Briefing • February 21–27, 2026
KEY THEMES
A quieter week for sponsor deal flow saw two digital infrastructure transactions, both announced Monday. Brookfield’s AI Infrastructure Fund acquired UK-based cloud operator Ori Industries and merged it with portfolio company Radiant, the first compute deployment vehicle from the fund’s $100B investment pipeline. Separately, SWI Stoneweg Icona Group deepened its US data center position with a $330M stake purchase, bringing total commitments to the underlying platform past $500M.
Digital 2 Deals
Ori Industries (Radiant) AI Cloud · UK
Acquirer: Brookfield (AI Infrastructure Fund)
Brookfield’s AI Infrastructure Fund acquired UK-based Ori Industries and merged it with portfolio company Radiant, creating what the firm described as the first vertically integrated AI infrastructure company. Radiant will operate 20-plus data centers worldwide using NVIDIA DSX architecture, targeting sovereign governments and enterprises under long-term contracts. The deal is the first compute deployment from the fund’s $100B AI infrastructure investment pipeline.
 
US Data Center Platform (Stake) Data Centers · US
Acquirer: SWI Stoneweg Icona Group
SWI Stoneweg Icona Group, a Euronext-listed alternative investment firm with €11B in assets under management, agreed to acquire additional interests in a US data center and digital infrastructure platform for $330M. Combined with a $170M option exercise earlier in February, SWI will hold approximately 77% of the $1.12B liquidation preference in the platform.
